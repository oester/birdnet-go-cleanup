"Species detection count" counts each species' matches across all pages of the other filters above (e.g. every nighttime detection in a date range), then keeps only the species whose total meets the condition — useful for finding rare/one-off misidentifications. Leave it on "(no filter)" for an ordinary search. Species summary shows one row per species with its count instead of every individual detection — combine it with a count condition for "just the rare species," or leave the count filter off to see counts for everything matching the other filters. Summary rows aren't selectable for delete; use "View detections" on a row to drop into the individual view for that species.
