"Species detection count" counts each species' matches across all pages of the other filters above (e.g. every nighttime detection in a date range), then keeps only the species whose total meets the condition — useful for finding rare/one-off misidentifications. Leave it on "(no filter)" for an ordinary search. Species summary shows one row per species with its count instead of every individual detection — combine it with a count condition for "just the rare species," or leave the count filter off to see counts for everything matching the other filters. Check summary rows to delete all of those species' detections within the current filters, or to add the species to the exclude list; use "View detections" on a row to review them individually first.
